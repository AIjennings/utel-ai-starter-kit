## 🛠️ OPTION B: The 60-Second Manual Setup (Windows File Explorer)

If you want to drop the safety file into your computer's AI folder yourself, follow the Word guide for your tool in this folder—or use this quick cheat sheet:

1. Download **`AGENTS.md`** from this Google Drive folder.
2. Open **Windows File Explorer**, click the address bar at the top, paste the path for your AI tool, and press **Enter**:
   * **Google Antigravity:** `%USERPROFILE%\.gemini`

3. *(If Windows says the folder doesn't exist, just create a new folder with that exact name—starting with the period).*
4. Drop **`AGENTS.md`** inside that folder.
5. Restart your AI tool and ask:
   > **"What persistent instructions are you following?"**

## 🚦 The 3 Golden Rules Your AI Now Follows Automatically

1. **Describe the Finished Kitchen (90/10 Rule):** You don't need technical prompts. Just tell your Chief of Staff what you want the finished output to look like and drop in your messy PDFs/notes. The AI builds the 90% draft; you own the final 10% approval.
2. **Traffic Light Data Safety:**
   * 🟢 **GREEN (Safe in Company AI):** Telecom bills, carrier quotes, contracts, call transcripts.
   * 🟡 **YELLOW (Internal Only):** Master agent / TSB commission splits & margins (Popeye strips these from client views).
   * 🔴 **RED (Never Paste):** Passwords, MFA codes, API keys, SSNs, bank routing, or credit cards.
3. **Drafts Only, Zero Guessing:** Your AI will **never** send an email, delete a file, or submit a carrier portal order on its own, and it must cite the exact page number for every dollar figure (`[Source: Doc, p. X]`).
