# Blueprint 04: Discovery Call Transcript → Client Follow-Up & Action Plan
**Workflow:** Paste a Zoom/Teams transcript or rough call notes → Get a client recap email draft + internal supplier/engineering checklist.

---

## Copy-Paste Prompt

```markdown
Act as a Senior Technology Advisor at Utel USA following our AGENTS.md security and citation standards.

### MY END-STATE VISION ("THE FINISHED KITCHEN"):
Below is the transcript / notes from a recent call. Turn this into two cleanly separated outputs:
1. A sharp **Client Follow-Up Email Draft** (`[DRAFT — FOR HUMAN APPROVAL]`) that makes the customer feel 100% heard and locks in next steps.
2. An **Internal Utel Execution Checklist** so our team and supplier engineers know exactly what quotes, LOAs, or bills to pull next.

### BUILD THESE 2 SECTIONS NOW:

#### PART 1: Internal Utel Action Plan (For Our Eyes Only)
- **Customer Pain Points & Decision Drivers:** What actually matters to them?
- **Technical Requirements Extracted:** Locations, bandwidths, seat counts, current carriers, contract end dates mentioned.
- **Missing Info Needed for Quoting:** What did we not get on the call that we still need?
- **Owner & Next Step Table:** `| Action Item | Owner | Due Date |`

#### PART 2: Client Recap Email (`[DRAFT — FOR HUMAN APPROVAL]`)
- Subject Line
- 3-sentence "What We Heard" summary
- Bullet list of what Utel USA is working on now
- Bullet list of what we need from them (e.g., latest carrier invoices, LOA, site list)
```
