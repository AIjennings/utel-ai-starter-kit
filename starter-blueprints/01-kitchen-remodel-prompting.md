# Blueprint 01: The "Kitchen Remodel" Prompting Method
**Concept by:** Tim Mackin (Utel USA)  
**Principle:** **Start With the End-State Vision — Not the Technical Steps**

---

## Why Most People Struggle With AI (And How to Fix It)

When most people open Claude, Antigravity, or ChatGPT, they think they need to talk like a computer programmer or write a 10-step technical instruction manual.

**You don't.**

Think about remodeling a kitchen:
- You don't walk up to a master contractor and tell them what gauge copper wire to run behind the wall or what brand of drywall screws to buy.
- Instead, **you describe what the finished kitchen looks like in a perfect world:** *"I want an open island where 6 people can sit, white quartz countertops, warm under-cabinet lighting, and a double oven next to the pantry."*

Once the contractor sees your **End-State Vision**, *they* figure out the plumbing, electrical, and framing—and then show you the blueprint for your approval before they swing a hammer.

---

## The 3-Sentence "Kitchen Remodel" Formula

Whenever you want AI to do something that gives you that **"holy sh*t, I can't believe it did that"** moment, just give it these three things:

1. **The Finished Kitchen (Your End State):** *"In a perfect world, here is what the finished output looks like and who is going to read it..."*
2. **The Raw Ingredients (What You Have Today):** *"Here are the messy files / bills / quotes / call notes I'm starting with..."*
3. **The Approval Gate (AI-First, Human-Approved):** *"Build the complete first draft now, cite the exact page numbers for every dollar figure, and flag anything you aren't 100% sure about at the bottom so I can approve it."*

---

## Copy-Paste Template (Drop This Into Any Chat or Agent)

```markdown
I want to use the Kitchen Remodel (End-State Vision) approach on this task.

1. THE FINISHED KITCHEN (WHAT I WANT AT THE END):
In a perfect world, I need [describe the final deliverable: e.g., a 1-page executive summary and side-by-side savings table for a CFO who only has 3 minutes to read it].

2. WHO IT IS FOR & WHY IT MATTERS:
This is for [Customer Name / Role]. Their biggest priority right now is [e.g., cutting legacy MPLS/POTS spend / upgrading redundancy / consolidating 14 locations onto one bill].

3. RAW INGREDIENTS (ATTACHED OR PASTED BELOW):
I am attaching/pasting [list what you dropped in: e.g., 3 carrier invoices, a Telarus fiber quote export, and my raw discovery notes].

4. RULES (AI-FIRST, HUMAN-APPROVED):
- Build the complete 90% finished deliverable right now—don't just give me an outline.
- Cite the exact source document and page/line for every dollar amount or contract term.
- Never guess a number; if something is missing, mark it [VERIFY NEEDED].
- At the very end, list the 3 highest-impact things I should double-check before I send this out.
```
